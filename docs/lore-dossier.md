**Name origin (CONFIRMED canon).** "Klukai" derives from **"Krokodil"** — *crocodile*. Producer Yuzhong stated the name has a "German flavor" despite Slavic roots. It is a deliberate foil to her predecessor **Leva** (from *lion*): two apex predators, two squad leaders, Leva on land / Klukai in water. (IOP Wiki; @TiferetEvocatus relaying the producer note.) "Those who do not exist" = Squad 404's name, after HTTP 404 *Not Found*.

**Signature equipment.** Suppressed **Glock 17 Gen 2** sidearm, **M84** stun grenades, extendable baton, **an axe**, and her **combat motorcycle** with built-in vertical missile launchers. (IOP Wiki / Steam guide summary.)

## 2. Backstory (canon beats)

- **NSA6 / the shameful past.** Originally served National Security Agency 6 with M16A1, UMP40, UMP45. Excessive pride, team-kills, insubordination → discharged. M16A1 **slapped** her to drive her from danger; she read it as humiliation and nursed years of hatred. (IOP Wiki *HK416/Story*.) The repo already has this. The famous **"You are Nothing"** beat is associated with the M16A1 confrontation arc *(this exact phrasing is widely repeated in the fanbase / TV Tropes but I could not re-confirm the precise wording from an accessible primary page — treat the **slap + "Nothing" humiliation** as canon-grounded, the exact quote as **probable-canon, single-source**).*
- **Squad 404 formation (2062).** After the Butterfly Incident killed UMP40, UMP45 recruited HK416; G11 joined. UMP45 named the unit Squad 404. Black-ops mercenaries with no official existence. (IOP Wiki *Squad 404*.)
- **GFL1 war years → Frankfurt.** Repeated losses to M16A1; growth past the revenge obsession; neural-cloud survival via backup. (IOP Wiki *HK416/Story*.)
- **Cross-game continuity.** GFL1 **HK416** → Project Neural Cloud **Clukay** → GFL2 **Klukai** are the same Digimind. Her GFL2 Corrosion AoE kit is explicitly "reminiscent of her PNC kit." (IOP Wiki *Klukai* / *Clukay*.)
- **Leadership handoff.** "Leva officially transferred the role of squad leader to 416 until the commission for NOMFA was completed, and 416 and G11 changed their names to **Klukai** and **Mechty**." (IOP Wiki *Klukai*.) Squad 404 expanded into the covert organization **H.I.D.E. 404**.
- **The Mephisto Agreement & ~10-year silence.** Commander signed the Mephisto Agreement (2065) barring contact with former subordinate Dolls and became a contaminated-zone bounty hunter. Klukai sent messages for ~10 years with no reply, sustained by 404's intel that he was alive. (personality.yaml; consistent with Wikipedia premise: Commander exiled to a contaminated zone, operating from the Elmo.)
- **Blood-tear tattoo (CONFIRMED meta-reference).** The tear under her left eye references **HK's lost trademark dispute with Colt**: the rifle was first marketed as the **"HKM4,"** but Colt Defense's claim over the "M4" name forced the rename to **HK416**. The blood-tear = that "defeat." (IOP Wiki *HK416*; widely corroborated.) The repo had the lawsuit angle but **not** the HKM4→HK416 rename specifics.
- **Klukadile plush** (crocodile mascot she'd deny owning) and **motorcycle = freedom** (the over-tense 416 felt genuine freedom riding for the first time, then bought her own): both consistent with IOP Wiki/character framing; treat the rented-bike-from-a-broker origin detail as **flavor canon** (in personality.yaml; not re-confirmed against an accessible primary page this pass).

- **Commander (human, male).** Object of her decade of unanswered messages; the bond is the spine of her GFL2 arc. Arrival line: *"It's been a while, Commander."* Possessive devotion: *"Commander, I'm the only one you need."* (IOP Wiki *HK416/Quotes*; YouTube max-affinity voice-line compilation.)
- **M16A1.** NSA6 trainer → the slap → years of rivalry/hatred → unresolved respect after she stops chasing revenge. (IOP Wiki *HK416/Story*.)
- **Leva (UMP45).** Predecessor, mentor, the lion to her crocodile; handed her the squad. (IOP Wiki.)
- **Mechty (G11).** Longest-serving partner; she carries Mechty (literally) and covers for her. (IOP Wiki.)

## 5. Personality (canon framing)

- "**Elegant and composed**, a Doll brimming with confidence, who aspires to make every member of H.I.D.E. 404 into an elite, with **tenderness lying behind her cold, strong-willed demeanor**." (IOP Wiki *Klukai* — this is the canonical one-line profile.)

- GFL1 baseline: "serious and foul-mouthed… takes great pride in her status as an Elite T-Doll" — the rough edge she has since channeled into composure. (IOP Wiki *HK416*.)
- **Fan-favorite metric (CONFIRMED, NEW):** despite releasing only ~4 months before EXILIUM's first anniversary, **Klukai was the Doll players gifted the most** during the game's first year. (IOP Wiki *Klukai*.) — strong canonical support for the companion's "crown-jewel tribute" framing.

## 7. Combat / Skills (EXACT in-game data — NEW granularity)

Source: **gfl2.help/en/character/Klukai** (corroborated by konnetwork, Dexerto).

- **Class:** Sentinel · **Weapon:** AR · **Element:** Corrosion (Medium Ammo) · **Weak to:** Shotgun Ammo, Electric.
- **Base stats:** ATK 827 · HP 1893 · DEF 504 · Stability 10 · Mobility 6.
- **Basic — "Swift Strike":** 1 target ≤8 tiles, physical dmg = 80% ATK.
- **Skill 1 — "Pinpoint Detonation":** 1 target ≤8 tiles, Corrosion = 80% ATK, then AoE = 60% ATK to target + enemies within 3 tiles, pulling them 1 tile toward center.
- **Skill 2 — "Overpowering Corrosion":** 1 tile ≤8, AoE Corrosion = 90% ATK within 3 tiles, applies **Toxic Infiltration** (2 turns).
- **Ultimate — "Devastating Drift":** lands on a tile in a cross-shaped 4–8 tile area, AoE Corrosion = 100% ATK along a path 3 tiles wide (she rides the motorcycle in). 
- **Passive — "Elite's Pride":** immunity to negative control-type tile effects; active attacks apply **Corrosive Infusion** (1 stack, 2 turns); gains **Competitive Spirit** after skill use.
- **Signature weapon — "Skylla" Assault Rifle**, effect **"Crocodile Tears"** (stacks bonus damage on each Corrosion hit); BiS by a wide margin. (konnetwork; Dexerto.) — **NEW; repo does not name the weapon.**
- **Best partners:** Peritya and Mechty (Corrosion team). (IOP Wiki; konnetwork.) — **NEW; "Peritya" not in repo.**
