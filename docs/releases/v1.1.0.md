# Klukai v1.1.0 — 2026-10-14

Klukai now supports Sonnet 5.5 with medium adaptive thinking as the interactive
primary, Dominus as the local secondary, and native Intel Arc XTTS voice.
Complete spoken replies, cancellation, and bounded voice jobs are included.

This release also prepares reference-conditioned OpenAI Images generation and
Sonnet dream/Her POV writing. **Cloud creative generation is disabled by default.**
Positive daily and monthly attempt limits, a key, and explicit provider selection
are required; images also require a character reference pack. Attempts are
reserved persistently before dispatch, including failed or uncertain requests.
These are request limits, not a hard dollar spending limit.

Dreams remain text entries. Portraits, inpainting, img2img, and ordinary background
jobs retain their local paths. Cloud historical backfill is refused. No paid image
or background dream call, identity comparison, or cloud-reference upload was made.
The owner's existing paid exception covers interactive Sonnet chat only.
