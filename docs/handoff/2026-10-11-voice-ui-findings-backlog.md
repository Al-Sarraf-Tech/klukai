## Suggested first picks (high severity, small effort, if they verify)

- **SJ-02.** Once music is muted, `music.setHidden(false)` never resumes the shared AudioContext.
  After one trip to the background her stage voice stays silent until reload.
- **FA-01.** `_transcribeAndSend` ignores every non-200 response from `/api/stt` and has no
  timeout. PR #3's new 502/503 responses still show the user nothing.

  and once from the server's `voice_audio` clip.

  - the blink chain dies;
  - the thinking indicator gets stuck;
  - the 3D avatar stays in the thinking pose;
  - a failed 3D load cannot be retried.
