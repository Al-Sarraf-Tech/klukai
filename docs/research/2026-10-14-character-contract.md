# Character contract evidence and implementation

The character should express earned confidence through judgment, practical care and protective leadership. This is an implementation synthesis of existing research and the owner's companion requirements, not a newly discovered game quotation.

This refactor used the dated character audit from 2026-10-14 (bus task `e084b5b558e9`, implementation `5180f89bb516`) and repository evidence. It made no external account, image, voice or paid calls. The audit reports reading [Klukai Profile](https://iopwiki.com/wiki/Klukai/Profile), [Quotes](https://iopwiki.com/wiki/Klukai/Quotes), [identity and roster material](https://iopwiki.com/wiki/Klukai), and [GFL2 Items](https://www.iopwiki.com/wiki/GFL2_Items). These are community transcriptions or reference pages, not publisher sources. Profile is marked incomplete. Different URLs on the same wiki are not independent corroboration.

Records 1–4 support matured protective leadership and the motorcycle origin. Record 5 supports years of unanswered correspondence, without establishing an exact daily or 0200 cadence. Record 7 supports practical gifts and fitted riding gear. Record 8 describes independent cooperation and a quarterly expense list. The application deliberately keeps its monthly settlement ritual; its cadence and original invoices are authored continuity. Reading invoices as a form of care is interpretation.

Exact timestamps, private wording and chronology placements in the app's Thread are authored continuity, even where an entry includes a localized line. The Thread still opens at its existing disclosure gates, and may still quote its stored entries. It must distinguish those entries from game records if asked. The item transcription attributes a Klukadile purchase to Belka; personal plush ownership and denial are treated as interpretation. Typology labels are interpretive, not diagnoses or canon. Player gifting and popularity results belong to game/community discussion, not automatic lived memory.

The audit's secondary community sources and the earlier [lore dossier](../lore-dossier.md) support the inherited Aphelion summary and anecdotes with weaker provenance. The dossier's former “new/not in repo” list is stale and is not a backlog. Team A/Team B and the existing roster are retained. A naming discrepancy on one wiki outfit list does not justify replacing the separately researched Indigo Oath name. Original outfit gates, the Commander's human identity and permanent appearance, and owner-selected voice source are preserved.

The legacy `voice_id` declaration was not used by any core consumer identified in the audit; it is not evidence of the active speech provider and remains outside this refactor. The [Japanese voice baseline](../../docker/voice/reference/README.md) remains the owner's reference. The audit successfully fetched subtitles but found translation artifacts; those subtitles are unsuitable for authoritative English quotation. Official X posts and a related publisher video were not successfully read during the audit. No primary-source content is reconstructed from memory, and this implementation does not upgrade those leads to verified evidence. The [source ledger](../../config/character_sources.yaml) records inherited evidence, interpretations and project choices separately.

## Supported prompt data

`identity.full_name`, former designation, organization, role, frame, weapon imprint, core motivation, simple wish and catchphrase are formatted directly. `biography_summary` and `voice_summary` are curated model-facing text. Long `backstory` and other catalog prose remain reference data; editing them alone deliberately does not alter the prompt. Aphelion's where/setup/inciting/obstacle/climax/coda fields and the silence arc's when/what fields are supported directly. Each curated quirk has a complete `prompt_summary`, disclosure condition, gate and source IDs. Python contains no parallel long biography, punctuation-based quirk truncation or source-ledger injection.

Minimal legacy fixtures retain a short identity preamble and configured user title. Missing optional summaries stay absent. Existing arc dictionaries keep their previous default gates; absent curated quirks produce no quirk block. Empty/invalid optional text is omitted rather than yielding an empty label. The public builder signatures remain compatible.

The response contract orders actual intent/results, departure/distress/context, stable identity/history, relationship/disclosure, then mood/style. It defines the ordinary Commander address and existing level-7+ private-context exception once. No universal sentence floor remains; jealousy cannot defeat departure or trust. Competence includes uncertainty, correction, truthful tool status and memory integrity.
